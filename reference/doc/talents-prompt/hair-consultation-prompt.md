SCENE: PERSONAL HAIR CONSULTATION

The talent sits in front of a large elegant salon mirror during a private consultation with a professional hairstylist.

The stylist stands slightly beside her, gently examining a section of her brunette hair and discussing potential cut, styling or color direction.

The talent turns naturally toward the camera so her complete face is clearly visible, maintaining calm eye contact with the lens.

Her reflection may appear subtly in the mirror but the primary visual focus remains her actual face.

The stylist should feel engaged and professional rather than posed.

Use an editorial medium shot showing enough of the salon station, mirror and consultation interaction to establish context.

Create an intimate personalized luxury-service atmosphere.
