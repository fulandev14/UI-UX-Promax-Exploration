## Objective

Communicate what it emotionally feels like to enter and spend time inside the salon.

The viewer should almost be able to imagine physically being there.

Focus on:

* interior architecture
* mirrors
* reflections
* furniture
* natural light
* materials
* spatial details
* intimate beauty moments

Strongly express:

**Nature Distilled × Cinematic Imagery × Boutique Hospitality**

Nature should appear through:

* stone
* wood
* linen
* water
* skin
* light
* shadow
* mineral colors
* tactile surfaces

Avoid literal botanical decoration.

Explore:

* full-bleed interior photography
* dramatic crops
* overlapping imagery
* atmospheric detail images
* large negative space
* typography integrated with architecture
* layered visual planes

The composition may visually suggest opportunities for subtle **Parallax Storytelling**, without defining technical implementation.

Communicate:

**calm + materiality + intimacy + comfort + sophistication**
