## Objective

Present the salon's primary services as a **curated collection rather than a conventional catalogue**.

Representative services may include:

* Hair Styling
* Hair Treatment
* Hair Coloring
* Scalp Ritual
* Beauty Treatment

Use actual service categories from the project when available.

Explore:

* oversized editorial numbering
* typography-led service indexing
* asymmetric image placement
* cinematic treatment imagery
* staggered compositions
* unusual image proportions
* controlled whitespace
* editorial hierarchy
* image + typography relationships

The section should communicate:

**expertise + craftsmanship + exclusivity + personal attention**

Avoid:

* three-card grids
* repetitive rounded cards
* icon + heading + paragraph patterns
* equal-sized service boxes
* generic pricing aesthetics

Services should feel **curated rather than catalogued**.

Different services may have different visual weight when this improves storytelling.

Typography and imagery should provide the primary visual language.
