# Create Brand Philosophy / About Visual Benchmark

Create a high-fidelity **Brand Philosophy / About Us Section visual benchmark**.

## Source of Truth

Read and analyze:

* `BRAND-GUIDELINES.md`
* `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`
* all previously approved section visual benchmarks

**Use all previously approved section visual benchmarks as continuity references. They are not templates to copy. Study them to preserve the established typography, color behavior, imagery treatment, spacing rhythm, grid logic, visual density, and overall art direction while creating a composition appropriate to the current section.**

Previously approved benchmarks establish visual continuity, not mandatory layout patterns.

Do not copy the Hero composition.

Create a composition specifically appropriate for Brand Philosophy / About while ensuring it unmistakably belongs to the same website.

Do not redesign the brand or established art direction.

Do not generate frontend code.

---

## Objective

Create a quieter editorial moment after the visual impact of the Hero.

This section should communicate the philosophy behind the salon rather than conventional corporate "About Us" information.

Communicate themes derived from `BRAND-GUIDELINES.md`, such as:

* craftsmanship
* personal care
* beauty as ritual
* intentionality
* refinement
* individuality
* comfort
* human expertise

Focus on:

* Editorial Grid / Magazine
* Minimal Swiss
* Asymmetric Layout
* typography-led composition
* generous negative space
* restrained imagery
* sophisticated reading hierarchy

Consider:

* oversized editorial statements
* narrow body-copy columns
* offset photography
* editorial numbering
* intentionally empty grid columns
* controlled image/text tension

Avoid conventional:

**IMAGE | ABOUT US TEXT**

and generic 50/50 compositions.

The pacing should deliberately transition:

**Hero = Dramatic**

↓

**About = Quiet + Intimate + Thoughtful**

It should feel like moving from the cover of a luxury magazine into its opening editorial.

Generate one high-fidelity visual benchmark for the complete Brand Philosophy / About section.
