# Create Footer Visual Benchmark

Create a high-fidelity **Footer Section visual benchmark**.

## Source of Truth

Read:

* `BRAND-GUIDELINES.md`
* `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`
* all previously approved section visual benchmarks

**Use all previously approved section visual benchmarks as continuity references. They are not templates to copy. Study them to preserve the established typography, color behavior, imagery treatment, spacing rhythm, grid logic, visual density, and overall art direction while creating a composition appropriate to the current section.**

Because this is the final section, study the complete visual journey established by all previously approved benchmarks.

The Footer should provide closure to that journey rather than introduce another visual direction.

Do not generate frontend code.

---

## Objective

Design the Footer as the final editorial composition of the landing page.

Do not treat it as a generic utility area.

Include representative:

* brand identity
* location
* opening hours
* contact
* social presence
* navigation
* booking
* legal information

Use:

* typography hierarchy
* editorial grid
* intentional negative space
* asymmetric alignment where appropriate
* restrained separators
* established brand colors
* sophisticated final composition

Consider one final oversized typographic or brand gesture to close the experience.

The transition:

**Booking CTA → Footer**

should feel intentional and visually resolved.

Avoid overly dense corporate multi-column footer patterns.

The Footer should simultaneously provide:

**utility + brand recognition + visual closure**

---

## Output

Generate one complete high-fidelity Footer visual benchmark.

Do not generate frontend code.

Do not proceed into website implementation.

Stop after generating the final visual artifact for review and approval.
