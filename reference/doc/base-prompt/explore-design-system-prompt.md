Saya ingin Anda membuat **beberapa alternatif visual direction yang benar-benar berbeda** untuk website **premium luxury beauty salon**.

Tujuan utama bukan langsung membuat website final. Fokus tahap ini adalah **visual exploration dan art direction** untuk menemukan arah desain terbaik sebelum implementasi.

## 1. Business Context

Website ditujukan untuk **high-end beauty salon** dengan positioning:
st
* Premium dan eksklusif
* Mahal tetapi terasa worth the price
* Comfortable, warm, calm, dan welcoming
* Sophisticated tanpa terlihat berlebihan
* Modern tetapi tidak terasa seperti SaaS/tech startup
* Memiliki strong brand personality
* Mengutamakan pengalaman pelanggan, craftsmanship, treatment quality, dan ambience
* Target customer menghargai quality, privacy, comfort, service, dan aesthetic experience

Website harus terasa seperti sebuah **luxury hospitality experience**, bukan sekadar website salon biasa.

# 3. Generate Multiple Visual Directions

Buat **6 visual directions**.

Setiap direction HARUS memiliki identitas visual yang berbeda secara signifikan.

Jangan hanya mengganti warna, font, atau gambar dari layout yang sama.

Eksplorasi harus berbeda dalam:

* composition
* layout philosophy
* typography treatment
* whitespace
* image placement
* section rhythm
* visual hierarchy
* navigation treatment
* interaction concept
* art direction
* photography treatment
* color strategy
* emotional character

Semua direction tetap harus mempertahankan positioning sebagai **premium luxury beauty salon**.

---

# 4. Suggested Visual Directions

Gunakan direction berikut sebagai titik awal eksplorasi, tetapi jangan membatasi kreativitas hanya pada deskripsi ini.

## Direction A — Swiss Editorial Luxury

Keywords:

`Swiss International Style, editorial beauty magazine, asymmetric grid, oversized grotesk typography, monochrome, disciplined whitespace, cinematic photography, high-fashion editorial`

Karakter:

Clean, intelligent, confident, highly curated.

Gunakan typography sebagai elemen visual utama.

Eksplorasi asymmetric grid, unusual typography placement, oversized headlines, dan dramatic whitespace.

---

## Direction B — Quiet Luxury Salon

Keywords:

`quiet luxury, understated elegance, warm minimalism, luxury hospitality, refined serif typography, neutral palette, tactile materials, calm editorial composition`

Karakter:

Warm, expensive, intimate, timeless.

Gunakan warna seperti:

* warm ivory
* bone
* sand
* taupe
* espresso
* charcoal

Photography harus terasa intimate dan cinematic.

Hindari visual luxury yang terlalu obvious.

---

## Direction C — Experimental Beauty Editorial

Keywords:

`experimental editorial, fashion magazine, beauty campaign, broken grid, oversized typography, unconventional composition, artistic cropping, high-fashion photography`

Karakter:

Bold, artistic, fashion-forward.

Gunakan:

* extreme typography scale
* asymmetric compositions
* cropped imagery
* overlapping elements
* unexpected whitespace
* editorial numbering
* unconventional section transitions

Direction ini boleh menjadi yang paling eksperimental.

---

## Direction D — Contemporary Spa Gallery

Keywords:

`contemporary art gallery, luxury spa, architectural minimalism, gallery whitespace, sculptural composition, serene photography, museum-inspired interface`

Karakter:

Calm, spacious, architectural.

Website harus terasa seperti kombinasi:

**luxury salon × contemporary gallery × boutique hotel**

Gunakan banyak negative space dan large-format imagery.

---

## Direction E — Dark Cinematic Luxury

Keywords:

`dark editorial luxury, cinematic beauty photography, black canvas, dramatic typography, sophisticated contrast, intimate lighting, luxury fashion campaign`

Karakter:

Exclusive, sensual, sophisticated, dramatic.

Gunakan dark palette secara restrained.

Hindari:

* cyberpunk
* neon
* gaming aesthetic
* tech startup aesthetic

Direction harus terasa seperti **luxury beauty campaign**, bukan nightclub.

---

## Direction F — Organic Modern Luxury

Keywords:

`organic modernism, wellness luxury, tactile minimalism, natural materials, warm editorial design, botanical influence, soft typography, premium self-care`

Karakter:

Comfortable, human, calming, premium.

Gunakan inspiration dari:

* stone
* linen
* wood
* cream
* skin tones
* natural light

Tetap pertahankan sophisticated editorial composition.

Jangan membuatnya terlihat seperti generic spa template.

# 5. Homepage Content Structure

Gunakan business content yang sama untuk seluruh visual direction agar perbandingan fokus pada desain.

Homepage minimal memiliki:

1. Navigation
2. Hero
3. Brand philosophy / introduction
4. Signature treatments
5. Salon experience
6. Selected services
7. Interior / atmosphere showcase
8. Beauty philosophy / craftsmanship
9. Testimonials atau social proof
10. Booking CTA
11. Footer

Anda boleh mengubah **urutan, composition, dan presentation** section untuk setiap direction jika menghasilkan storytelling yang lebih kuat.

Jangan memaksakan semua section menjadi rectangular container.

Setiap section harus diperlakukan sebagai **art-directed canvas**.

---

# 6. Typography

Typography merupakan salah satu elemen terpenting.

Eksplorasi kombinasi:

* oversized grotesk display
* Swiss neo-grotesk
* elegant editorial serif
* high-contrast serif
* modern grotesk
* restrained italic serif
* oversized editorial numerals

Gunakan typography sebagai bagian dari composition, bukan hanya sebagai text container.

Headline boleh menggunakan:

* dramatic line breaks
* asymmetric alignment
* extreme scale contrast
* intentional letter spacing
* vertical positioning
* typography/image interaction

Tetap prioritaskan readability.

---

# 7. Photography Direction

Photography harus terasa seperti **premium campaign photography**, bukan stock photography.

Gunakan visual direction:

* cinematic salon interiors
* editorial beauty portraits
* close-up hair texture
* hands performing treatments
* subtle product details
* natural skin texture
* material close-ups
* mirrors
* reflections
* salon architecture
* soft natural lighting
* controlled studio lighting

Gunakan:

**full-bleed imagery + oversized editorial crops + intentional negative space.**

Hindari gambar salon stock generik dengan model tersenyum langsung ke kamera.

---

# 8. Motion Direction

Desain harus mempertimbangkan kemungkinan implementasi motion.

Eksplorasi:

* smooth scrolling
* typography reveal
* mask image reveal
* subtle parallax
* sticky editorial sections
* horizontal storytelling
* image scale transitions
* staggered typography
* cinematic page transitions

Motion harus:

**slow, intentional, sophisticated, restrained.**

Jangan membuat setiap elemen bergerak.

---

# 9. Anti-Patterns

JANGAN menghasilkan desain seperti generic AI-generated landing page.

Hindari:

* generic SaaS layout
* centered hero + CTA + three cards
* repetitive card grids
* excessive rounded cards
* excessive border radius
* glassmorphism
* glowing UI
* purple/blue AI gradients
* gradient-heavy backgrounds
* generic Bootstrap sections
* excessive pills
* excessive badges
* floating dashboard cards
* icon-heavy feature sections
* generic stock illustrations
* repetitive alternating image/text sections
* identical section spacing everywhere
* overly symmetrical composition
* generic luxury gold-on-black aesthetic

Khusus luxury:

**Do not equate luxury with gold gradients, excessive serif fonts, ornaments, or decorative flourishes.**

Luxury harus datang dari:

**proportion, typography, photography, materials, whitespace, composition, restraint, and attention to detail.**

---

# 10. Exploration Requirement

Saya ingin keenam direction terlihat seperti dibuat oleh **6 creative directors yang berbeda** dengan brief bisnis yang sama.

Perbedaan harus terlihat bahkan jika seluruh direction dibuat grayscale.

Artinya differentiation tidak boleh bergantung hanya pada warna.

Prioritaskan perbedaan pada:

**layout + typography + composition + imagery + whitespace + rhythm.**
