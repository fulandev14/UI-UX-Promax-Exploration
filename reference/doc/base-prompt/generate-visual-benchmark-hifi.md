# Create a Full-Page Visual Benchmark from the Approved Design System

Use the existing **`DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`** as the primary and authoritative source of truth for this task.

Your task is to translate the approved design system into a **complete, high-fidelity, full-page visual benchmark** for a premium luxury beauty salon website.

## IMPORTANT — VISUAL DESIGN ONLY

This phase is strictly for:

**Visual Design Exploration → Full-Page Visual Benchmark → Review → Revision → Approval**

Do NOT proceed into frontend development or technical implementation.

Do NOT generate production code.

Do NOT shift the task toward technical architecture.

The purpose of this phase is to establish and validate the **visual quality, composition, art direction, storytelling, and overall experience** before any implementation phase begins.

---

Create a complete **full-page homepage visual benchmark** that demonstrates how `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` should look when fully realized as a premium digital experience.

# 3. Creative DNA That Must Be Visible

The benchmark must visibly express all of the following principles:

### 1. Asymmetric Layout

Use intentional visual imbalance, offset positioning, unconventional column relationships, and controlled negative space.

Asymmetry must feel deliberate and sophisticated.

---

### 2. Cinematic Imagery

Photography should create atmosphere and emotion.

Images must feel like frames from a premium beauty campaign, fashion editorial, or cinematic brand film.

---

### 3. Art Direction

Every major section must feel individually composed.

Avoid the impression that the page was assembled from reusable website templates.

---

### 4. Immersive Interactive Storytelling

The page should visually communicate a journey.

Scrolling through the conceptual page should feel like progressing through a carefully curated beauty experience.

---

### 5. Editorial Grid / Magazine

Use magazine-inspired composition, strong typography, image cropping, intentional whitespace, editorial numbering, and structured grid relationships.

---

### 6. Parallax Storytelling

Where appropriate, visually indicate opportunities for layered depth and subtle parallax relationships between imagery, typography, and background elements.

Do not make parallax the dominant visual gimmick.

---

### 7. Nature Distilled

Express nature through:

* materials
* texture
* light
* shadow
* stone
* linen
* wood
* skin
* water
* mineral tones
* subtle organic forms

Avoid literal or cliché botanical decoration.

---

### 8. Bold Typography Mobile

The visual system must preserve bold editorial typography on smaller compositions.

Typography should remain expressive rather than becoming timid or generic.

---

### 9. Minimal Swiss

Use disciplined alignment, typography, grid structure, restraint, clarity, and functional simplicity as the underlying foundation.

---

### 10. Scroll-Triggered Storytelling

Visually identify moments where content could reveal, transition, or evolve as the visitor progresses through the narrative.

The visual benchmark should communicate these ideas without implementing them technically.

---

### 11. Horizontal Scroll Journey

Include one meaningful visual sequence designed around a horizontal narrative.

It should serve storytelling rather than decoration.

# 28. Visual Benchmark Validation

After creating the visual benchmark, critically evaluate it against the design system.

Ask:

### Art Direction

Does every major section feel intentionally composed?

### Typography

Would the design remain visually distinctive even without photography?

### Asymmetric Layout

Is asymmetry deliberate and balanced?

### Editorial Grid

Can the underlying grid discipline still be perceived?

### Cinematic Imagery

Does photography participate in storytelling?

### Nature Distilled

Does nature appear through materiality and atmosphere rather than cliché decoration?

### Storytelling

Does moving visually through the page feel like progressing through a narrative?

### Horizontal Journey

Does the horizontal sequence have a meaningful storytelling purpose?

### Visual Rhythm

Does the page alternate successfully between quiet and dramatic moments?

### Luxury

Does luxury come from restraint, proportion, typography, imagery, and composition rather than decoration?

### Mobile

Does the mobile direction retain the personality of the desktop experience?

### Distinctiveness

Could this visual benchmark easily be mistaken for a premium salon template?

If yes, the benchmark is not distinctive enough.

Refine the composition.
