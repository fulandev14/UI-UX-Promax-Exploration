# Grid Continuity

# Skin & Beauty Rendering

Maintain the approved natural photographic character.

Skin should feel:

**soft + natural + smooth + healthy + subtly textured**

Avoid:

* hyper-sharp pores
* excessive micro-texture
* plastic skin
* aggressive beauty retouching
* excessive sharpening
* HDR skin
* artificial gloss
* overexposure

Hair should remain detailed and tactile without looking artificially sharpened.

The photographic treatment should feel sophisticated and editorial rather than hyper-real digital rendering.

# Avoid the "Collage Problem"

The final output must NOT look like:

**10 individually beautiful designs stacked vertically.**

It must look like:

**one website designed from beginning to end by the same creative director.**

This distinction is critical.

Look specifically for:

* abrupt background changes
* inconsistent image grading
* inconsistent typography scale
* inconsistent margins
* unrelated grid behavior
* duplicated visual patterns
* excessive visual density
* sudden style changes
* inconsistent model appearance
* disconnected section boundaries

Resolve these issues while preserving the approved character of each section.

---

# Do Not Over-Harmonize

Do not solve continuity problems by making every section visually identical.

Avoid turning the entire page into one repetitive system.

Preserve intentional contrast.

The goal is:

**Consistency without repetition.**

**Variation without fragmentation.**

**Experimentation without chaos.**

**Luxury without decoration.**

Before finalizing, evaluate the complete page from top to bottom.

Ask:

### Brand

Does everything clearly belong to the same brand?

### Art Direction

Does the website feel directed as one complete visual experience?

### Typography

Is hierarchy consistent throughout the page?

### Grid

Is there an underlying editorial structure connecting sections?

### Photography

Does all imagery belong to the same photographic universe?

### Talent

Do recurring models remain visually consistent?

### Color

Does the palette transition naturally?

### Spacing

Does vertical rhythm feel intentional?

### Storytelling

Does each section naturally lead into the next?

### Visual Density

Are dramatic and quiet sections appropriately balanced?

### Distinctiveness

Does the complete website still retain the character of the approved section benchmarks?

### Cohesion

Does this look like one website rather than multiple visual concepts stitched together?

If not, refine the transitions and continuity.
