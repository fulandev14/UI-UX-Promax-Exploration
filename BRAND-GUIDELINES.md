# Atelier Élan — Brand Guidelines

**Status:** Brand identity foundation · **Version:** 1.0  
**Authoritative inputs:** approved visual benchmarks in `reference/approved/` and `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`  
**Purpose:** guide designers, writers, marketers, photographers, creative directors, and salon teams without diluting the approved identity.

---

## 1. Brand Overview

Atelier Élan is a private, high-end beauty atelier where beauty is treated as craft, ritual, atmosphere, and personal expression. It offers the expertise of a master salon, the intimacy of a private studio, and the cultural sensibility of a contemporary editorial brand.

The brand is not defined by decoration or status signals. Its luxury is experienced through close attention, precise technique, thoughtful pacing, natural materiality, and a result that feels personal rather than imposed.

**Brand idea:** Beauty is not added. It is carefully revealed.

## 2. Brand Positioning

### Category

A premium private beauty atelier specializing in intentional hair craft, color, scalp care, and restorative beauty rituals.

### Market position

Atelier Élan sits above premium multi-chair salons and below inaccessible couture spectacle. It combines luxury-level expertise with boutique hospitality and one-to-one attention. It is more personal than a luxury salon, more culturally articulate than a wellness brand, and more service-grounded than a fashion label.

### Audience

Design-aware clients who value expertise, discretion, calm, natural-looking results, and a relationship with an artist who understands their identity. They are willing to pay more for judgment, consistency, time, and care—not for ceremony alone.

### Value proposition

Atelier Élan creates enduring, individual beauty through expert consultation, precise craft, considered care, and a private atmosphere in which every detail has a reason.

### Emotional value

- **Before:** understood, reassured, and quietly anticipated.
- **During:** present, comfortable, cared for, and unhurried.
- **After:** recognizably themselves—refined, restored, and confident.

### Functional value

- One-to-one consultation and attention.
- Expert technique adapted to movement, texture, tone, and lifestyle.
- Carefully paced service with no assembly-line feeling.
- Durable results and clear maintenance guidance.
- A consistent environment designed around comfort and concentration.

### Differentiation

Most premium salons communicate trend, glamour, or indulgence. Atelier Élan communicates discernment. It edits rather than transforms, listens before prescribing, and treats atmosphere as part of the service—not as decoration around it.

### Positioning statement

> For design-aware clients who want beauty to feel personal rather than performed, Atelier Élan is a private beauty atelier combining precise craft, considered ritual, and intimate hospitality to create results that feel natural, enduring, and unmistakably their own.

## 3. Brand Essence

### **Intimate Editorial Craft**

**Intimate** means personal attention, comfort, discretion, and human presence.  
**Editorial** means discernment, cultural confidence, visual tension, and a clear point of view.  
**Craft** means expertise, precision, material understanding, and results that endure.

Use this essence as a decision filter: if an experience is impersonal, visually generic, or technically careless, it is not Atelier Élan.

## 4. Brand Purpose

Atelier Élan exists to make personal care feel genuinely personal—to create the time, expertise, and atmosphere in which people can recognize and refine what already feels true to them.

## 5. Brand Promise

> **Your full self, met with full attention.**

Every interaction should deliver expert judgment, honest guidance, unhurried care, a calm environment, and a result designed to live well beyond the appointment.

## 6. Brand Personality

### Refined

- **Means:** precise, considered, and sophisticated.
- **Visually:** disciplined grids, controlled type contrast, restrained palette.
- **Verbally:** clear sentences and exact claims.
- **Never:** pretentious, elitist, or ornate.

### Intimate

- **Means:** attentive, discreet, and personally present.
- **Visually:** close crops, hands, skin, texture, and quiet reading space.
- **Verbally:** direct human language that speaks to one person.
- **Never:** intrusive, overfamiliar, or sentimental.

### Artistic

- **Means:** culturally aware, composed, and expressive.
- **Visually:** editorial asymmetry, bold typography, cinematic sequencing.
- **Verbally:** memorable phrasing used with restraint.
- **Never:** chaotic, obscure, or self-indulgent.

### Assured

- **Means:** expert, calm, and decisive.
- **Visually:** strong scale, clear hierarchy, deliberate negative space.
- **Verbally:** active voice and specific recommendations.
- **Never:** loud, boastful, or absolute without proof.

### Tactile

- **Means:** grounded in material, touch, light, and physical experience.
- **Visually:** stone, linen, wood, water, hair, skin, and visible texture.
- **Verbally:** sensory detail tied to a real experience.
- **Never:** rustic, earthy as a trend, or decorated with botanical clichés.

### Calm

- **Means:** paced, comfortable, and emotionally controlled.
- **Visually:** breathing room, quiet chapters, natural tonal transitions.
- **Verbally:** concise, reassuring, and never urgent without reason.
- **Never:** empty, passive, clinical, or vague.

### Individual

- **Means:** attentive to the person rather than a prescribed ideal.
- **Visually:** distinctive faces, natural textures, and non-generic expression.
- **Verbally:** “your” is used to recognize context, not manufacture intimacy.
- **Never:** trend-led, corrective, or based on beauty anxiety.

## 9. Brand Story

Beauty does not need to be rushed into a new shape. It needs time, attention, and someone who knows how to look closely.

Atelier Élan was conceived as an alternative to the volume and spectacle of conventional salons: a private place where consultation comes before prescription, technique serves the individual, and the atmosphere gives both artist and client room to be present.

Here, a cut is considered in movement, color is designed to live and grow, and care extends beyond the appointment. Stone, linen, wood, water, skin, and light create a setting that feels grounded rather than staged.

The result is not a performed version of beauty. It is a clearer expression of the person already there.

## 10. Narrative Pillars

### 01 — Craft

Expertise, precision, judgment, and the intelligence of the hand. Use for artist profiles, process, education, services, and behind-the-scenes content.

### 02 — Individuality

Beauty shaped around texture, movement, identity, and daily life. Use for consultation stories, client narratives, and tailored recommendations.

### 03 — Ritual

Personal care as meaningful time rather than indulgent theatre. Use for appointment journeys, scalp care, restorative treatments, and hospitality.

### 04 — Materiality

The sensory world of hair, skin, linen, stone, water, wood, and light. Use for interiors, product stories, print, packaging, and atmospheric campaigns.

### 05 — Endurance

Results, relationships, and choices designed to last. Use for maintenance education, responsible practice, long-term care, and client trust.

## 11. Brand Voice

The Atelier Élan voice is **calm, assured, intimate, sensory, intelligent, and concise**.

It speaks as an experienced artist with nothing to prove: observant enough to notice detail, confident enough to be direct, and warm enough to make expertise feel comfortable.

### Voice attributes

- **Clear before clever.** Meaning is never sacrificed for an editorial phrase.
- **Specific before superlative.** Describe the craft, material, or result.
- **Sensory but controlled.** Touch, texture, movement, shadow, and light support meaning.
- **Personal but respectful.** Speak to the individual without assuming insecurity.
- **Confident but quiet.** Avoid shouting, urgency, and self-congratulation.

## 12. Tone of Voice

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Brand campaign | Emotional, editorial, distilled | “Beauty, made tangible.” |
| Homepage | Assured, atmospheric, concise | “Where artistry meets intention.” |
| Service description | Clear, expert, outcome-led | “Dimensional color designed for a softer grow-out.” |
| Consultation | Curious, attentive, reassuring | “Tell us how your hair moves through an ordinary week.” |
| Booking | Direct, calm, practical | “Choose a service and a time. We’ll confirm the details personally.” |
| Customer support | Warm, transparent, solution-focused | “Your appointment has moved successfully. Here is the new time.” |
| Social media | Observational, immediate, still refined | “The final line is shaped after the hair falls naturally.” |
| Editorial journal | Intelligent, descriptive, culturally aware | “Why longevity begins with the consultation.” |
| Error or disruption | Honest, calm, accountable | “We couldn’t complete your booking. Your selection is saved; please try again.” |

## 13. Writing Principles

1. **Lead with one idea.** One headline should carry one clear thought.
2. **Write with precision.** Replace “amazing results” with the specific result and why it lasts.
3. **Use active voice.** “We shape the cut around movement,” not “The cut is shaped.”
4. **Be concise.** Editorial language needs space around it.
5. **Make sensory language useful.** Texture, weight, movement, warmth, and light must describe an actual quality.
6. **Respect the reader.** Never manufacture anxiety or imply they need fixing.
7. **Balance poetry with information.** Expressive headlines; clear service details, prices, policies, and instructions.
8. **Prefer quiet confidence.** Avoid exclamation marks, all-caps paragraphs, hype, and false scarcity.
9. **Use sentence case for prose.** Uppercase is a visual role for short labels, not the default writing voice.

### Before / after

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| “Experience the ultimate luxury transformation.” | “A considered cut, shaped to move with you.” |
| “Reveal your flawless beauty.” | “Texture, tone, and shape—carefully refined.” |
| “Book now before spots are gone!” | “Reserve your appointment.” |
| “Our world-class stylists deliver perfect results.” | “Expert technique. Full attention. A result made to last.” |

## 15. Messaging Hierarchy

1. **Brand essence — Intimate Editorial Craft:** internal filter for all decisions.
2. **Brand promise — Your full self, met with full attention:** customer expectation across every interaction.
3. **Value proposition:** explains why the premium is justified.
4. **Narrative pillars:** organize ongoing content and campaigns.
5. **Campaign messages:** one emotionally resonant idea expressed through image and headline.
6. **Service messages:** outcome + method + relevant practical detail.
7. **Functional communication:** direct instructions, confirmations, policies, and recovery paths.

Higher levels create meaning; lower levels create clarity. Never force campaign poetry into functional communication.

## 17. Visual Identity Principles

### Disciplined asymmetry

The brand is personal rather than standardized. Uneven proportions, offsets, and controlled overlap communicate individuality, while Swiss grid anchors preserve confidence and legibility.

### Typography as presence

Monumental grotesk headlines create cultural confidence. The editorial serif introduces intimacy and emotional contrast. Mono labels express craft, sequence, and precision.

### Cinematic proximity

Faces, hair, hands, tools, and interiors are observed closely. Photography is not decoration; it carries emotion, proof, and narrative.

### Luxury through restraint

Warm paper, ink, material neutrals, negative space, and precise scale replace ornament, metallic effects, and conventional luxury signals.

### Controlled disruption

Torn seams, vertical labels, oversized numerals, and off-grid captions create recognition. Each composition uses only one dominant disruption.

### Quiet-to-dramatic rhythm

The brand alternates between intimacy and impact. Dense editorial scenes require quiet space before or after them.

### Core brand colors

| Color | Value | Brand role |
|---|---|---|
| Warm Ivory | `#FBF8F1` | Primary field; calm, paper, hospitality, space. |
| Charcoal | `#181816` | Main typography; confidence without hard digital black. |
| Ink | `#0E0E0D` | Inverse chapters and dramatic narrative punctuation. |
| Acid Chartreuse | `#D7ED18` | Surgical signal for booking, active progress, or one editorial annotation. |

### Supporting neutrals

Soft Cream `#F4EFE5`, Bone `#E7DFD1`, Stone `#C9BDAD`, Taupe `#74685D`, Espresso `#2B241E`.

These colors create hierarchy, warmth, material continuity, and quiet transitions. They should not collapse into undifferentiated beige; ink, type scale, rules, and crop provide structure.

### Material colors

Clay `#765745`, Walnut `#4B392D`, and Moss `#69705A` connect the identity to physical surfaces. Moss is muted atmosphere—not a wellness-brand green.

### Balance

- Ivory/cream/bone fields dominate.
- Ink/charcoal provide decisive structure and occasional full inverse chapters.
- Material neutrals bridge photography and typography.
- Chartreuse occupies a very small visual area and must retain its rarity.

### Avoid

- Chartreuse as a large decorative background.
- Gold, rose gold, glossy black, pink, lavender, or generic beauty gradients.
- Low-contrast taupe body copy.
- More than one competing accent in a composition.

## 19. Typography Identity

Exact roles, scale, line height, and responsive behavior remain in `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`.

### Primary grotesk — Inter / approved neo-grotesk stack

Represents confidence, precision, modernity, and Swiss discipline. Use for wordmark direction, display headlines, navigation, service titles, functional UI, and clear body copy. Monumental scale is a brand asset, especially on mobile.

### Editorial serif — Bodoni Moda / approved high-contrast serif stack

Represents intimacy, elegance, craft, and emotional inflection. Use for one expressive phrase, quotations, selected headlines, and oversized editorial numbers. Never use it for navigation, pricing, form guidance, or long instructional copy.

### Mono — IBM Plex Mono / approved monospace stack

Represents process, detail, sequence, and the language of an atelier. Use for issue labels, captions, prices, chapter numbers, dates, and image metadata.

### Recognition rule

Brand recognition comes from the tension between monumental grotesk, selective serif, and precise mono—not from any one typeface alone. Preserve the hierarchy if substitutes are required.

## 20. Photography Identity

Photography should feel observed, not staged; intimate, not invasive; refined, not retouched beyond recognition.

| Category | Mood and light | Composition | Subject behavior and texture | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Human | Calm, self-possessed, warm directional light. | Off-center 4:5, 3:4, or 2:3 portraits; close but breathable. | Natural skin, expressive hair, subtle gaze, quiet movement. | Generic smiles, glamour posing, airbrushed skin. |
| Craft | Focused, tactile, deep detailed shadows. | Hands/tools as focal point; diagonals and extreme detail crops. | Authentic technique, visible strands, fingertips preserved. | Fake cutting, staged tool arrangements, clinical brightness. |
| Space | Intimate hospitality, late natural light. | Architectural depth, mirrors, thresholds, 16:9–21:9 spreads. | Real material surfaces, one-chair privacy, lived-in restraint. | Sterile showrooms, artificial opulence, gold fixtures. |
| Material | Quiet, abstract, raking light. | 1:1, 5:4, or 9:16 studies with generous negative space. | Stone, linen, water, walnut, ceramic, skin-like shadow. | Leaves, flowers, candles, stacked stones. |
| Atmosphere | Observational, cinematic, emotionally controlled. | Reflections, blur, foreground obstruction, shadow sequences. | Subtle motion and tonal depth. | Heavy filters, lens-effect novelty, empty mood without story. |

### Cropping principles

- Protect eyes, fingertips, tools, and the direction of gaze.
- Crop to intensify attention, not conceal weak imagery.
- Alternate scale: portrait → craft detail → material/space → result.
- Preserve intentional negative space for typography.
- Mobile crops are authored independently; they are not automatic center crops.

## 21. Cinematic Imagery

Cinematic does not mean dark, dramatic, or expensive-looking by default. It means every image behaves like a purposeful frame in a larger sequence.

- Light establishes time, atmosphere, and emotional temperature.
- Composition suggests what happened before and what follows.
- Close detail provides proof; wide space provides context.
- Expression is subtle and emotionally controlled.
- Grain may add tactility at 2–4% but never obscure texture or skin.
- Retouching preserves pores, strands, fabric, stone, and honest tonal variation.

**Sequence rule:** human presence → craft → material → environment → result. Vary the sequence when the narrative requires it, but never use unrelated beautiful images as filler.

## 22. Nature Distilled

Nature Distilled is a method of abstraction, not a decorating theme.

- **Color:** mineral neutrals, warm earth, ink, muted moss.
- **Material:** linen, honed stone, walnut, ceramic, water, paper.
- **Photography:** natural side light, shadow, tactile detail, subtle movement.
- **Interior:** honest surfaces and architectural calm.
- **Print:** uncoated paper, blind emboss, hairline rules, restrained ink coverage.
- **Packaging:** material-conscious, low-gloss, minimal layers, texture over ornament.
- **Campaigns:** nature appears through atmosphere and physical truth, never literal leaves or wellness symbolism.

## 23. Graphic Language

Use only devices already evident in the approved work:

- Editorial numbers `01–05` as sequence and navigation.
- Thin rules and grid lines as structure.
- Vertical labels and rotated metadata as controlled interruption.
- Torn or irregular paper seams as a rare campaign/hero device.
- Oversized cropped letterforms and numerals.
- Mono captions, prices, issue labels, and credits.
- Sharp image boundaries and mixed aspect ratios.
- Paper/film grain at restrained opacity.
- Chartreuse brush or line accent, limited to one focal signal.

Do not add decorative icons, patterns, illustrations, badges, or symbols without a functional narrative role.

## 24. Logo Direction / Usage

No standalone final logo asset is approved. The benchmarks establish a provisional uppercase **ATELIER ÉLAN** wordmark treatment; do not mistake generated benchmark lettering for production-ready logo artwork.

### Future logo direction brief

- A refined wordmark is the primary need.
- Character should be architectural, restrained, editorial, and legible.
- It may draw from the approved grotesk or a carefully customized transitional/editorial letterform, but must coexist with monumental headlines rather than compete with them.
- A monogram is optional and should be developed only for genuinely small or square touchpoints.
- No beauty icon, face silhouette, flower, scissors, mirror, crown, script flourish, or ornamental crest.

### Interim wordmark rules

- Use the text “ATELIER ÉLAN” exactly, including the accent.
- Uppercase with measured tracking; never condensed until illegible.
- Dark version on ivory/light material fields; reversed ivory version on ink/charcoal.
- Place on quiet image zones only; use a solid field when contrast is uncertain.
- Clear space: at least the cap height of the wordmark on all sides.
- Minimum suggested width: 120px digital, 35mm print.
- Never stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, gradient-fill, crop, or combine with an unapproved symbol.

## 26. Brand Consistency & Creative Freedom

### Fixed brand elements

- Brand essence, purpose, promise, and personality.
- Core palette and chartreuse scarcity.
- Grotesk × serif × mono typographic relationship.
- Photography philosophy and honest texture.
- Disciplined asymmetry and negative-space character.
- Voice: calm, assured, concise, human, sensory.
- Nature Distilled as material abstraction.
- Luxury through restraint.

### Flexible brand elements

- Exact editorial composition and section order.
- Image scale, crop, and sequence.
- Ratio of quiet to dramatic space by medium.
- Serif phrase placement and oversized-number usage.
- Supporting material neutral selected for a campaign.
- Narrative pillar emphasized.

### Experimental elements

- Controlled torn-paper seams and physical collage.
- Motion choreography and layered depth.
- Campaign-specific typographic collisions.
- Horizontal or sequential storytelling formats.
- Photographic blur, reflection, and foreground obstruction.

Experimental work must retain one clear grid anchor, one dominant disruption, approved palette behavior, and complete information access.

## 27. Brand Guardrails

### Always

Intentional, refined, editorial, cinematic, human, tactile, restrained, assured, personal, legible, materially grounded, and carefully paced.

### Never

Generic luxury, clinical, excessively feminine, ornamental, flashy, corporate, technology-coded, trend-chasing, generic spa, influencer beauty, corrective, or anxiety-driven.

### Never rely on

- Gold or metallic gradients.
- Script typography and ornamental monograms.
- Flower, leaf, candle, or stacked-stone imagery.
- Excessive beige without hierarchy or contrast.
- Generic “premium/luxury/exclusive” vocabulary.
- Rounded card systems, glassmorphism, glows, or pill-heavy layouts.
- Retouched perfection, generic smiling models, or salon stock photography.
- Urgency, discounting language, or manufactured scarcity as the dominant conversion tool.

## 28. Brand Recognition Test

A brand expression passes without the logo or salon name when at least five of these seven signals remain recognizable:

1. Monumental grotesk with selective high-contrast serif.
2. Precise mono labels and oversized editorial numbering.
3. Warm paper/ink palette with rare chartreuse signal.
4. Disciplined asymmetric grid and active negative space.
5. Intimate cinematic sequence of human, craft, material, and space.
6. Nature expressed through material, light, and texture.
7. Calm, specific, sensory voice focused on attention and enduring craft.

If photography changes, preserve crop behavior, light, texture, and sequence. If format changes, preserve hierarchy, restraint, and one controlled disruption. If content changes, preserve the voice and narrative pillars.

## 30. Practical Brand Checklist

### Strategy

- [ ] Does the work express Intimate Editorial Craft?
- [ ] Is the benefit specific to attention, craft, ritual, individuality, materiality, or endurance?
- [ ] Does it justify premium value through evidence rather than status language?

### Voice and messaging

- [ ] Is the message calm, assured, concise, human, and specific?
- [ ] Is expressive copy separated from functional instruction?
- [ ] Does sensory language describe something real?
- [ ] Are perfection, anxiety, hype, and generic luxury claims absent?
- [ ] Is the CTA direct and respectful?

### Visual identity

- [ ] Does the work use only approved color and typography roles?
- [ ] Is chartreuse rare and purposeful?
- [ ] Is asymmetry anchored to a disciplined grid?
- [ ] Is there one dominant visual gesture rather than several competing effects?
- [ ] Does negative space create hierarchy and pacing?
- [ ] Are sharp boundaries, rules, and scale doing more work than cards or decoration?

### Photography

- [ ] Does imagery feel observational, intimate, tactile, and cinematic?
- [ ] Are skin, hair, material, and light rendered honestly?
- [ ] Do crops protect eyes, fingertips, tools, and gaze direction?
- [ ] Does the image contribute to a sequence or story?
- [ ] Are stock-salon, spa, botanical, glamour, and over-retouched clichés absent?

### Logo / wordmark

- [ ] Is the interim wordmark reproduced exactly with correct accent and clear space?
- [ ] Is contrast sufficient and placement quiet?
- [ ] Is it free from effects, distortion, and unapproved symbols?

### Accessibility and integrity

- [ ] Does text meet the contrast requirements in `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`?
- [ ] Is information understandable without color, photography, or motion?
- [ ] Are service claims, prices, availability, and policies accurate?
- [ ] Are image rights, model releases, credits, and usage permissions documented?

### Final recognition check

- [ ] If the name and wordmark disappear, do at least five of the seven recognition signals remain?
- [ ] Could this be mistaken for a generic premium salon, spa, or beauty influencer brand?
- [ ] If yes, strengthen craft, editorial tension, specificity, and material intelligence before approval.
